🛡️ Running a lightweight ISO 27001 gap analysis

31 Jul 2026 — Work

Information SecurityISO 27001

When I start a gap analysis, I don't reach for the full standard text on day one. Instead I work from a condensed checklist mapped to Annex A themes, and note down where evidence already exists versus where it needs to be created.

Why this works

  • It keeps early conversations with process owners short and concrete
  • It surfaces "quick win" controls that are already partially in place
  • It leaves the deep-dive reading for the areas that actually need it

More detail on the tooling I use for this coming in a future post.
