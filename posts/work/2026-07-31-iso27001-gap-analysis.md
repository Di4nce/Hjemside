When I start a gap analysis, I don't reach for the full standard text on day one.
Instead I work from a condensed checklist mapped to Annex A themes, and note down
where evidence already exists versus where it needs to be created.

## Why this works

- It keeps early conversations with process owners short and concrete
- It surfaces "quick win" controls that are already partially in place
- It leaves the deep-dive reading for the areas that actually need it
