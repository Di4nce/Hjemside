The party spent most of tonight's session mapping the flooded lower level of the
keep before the fighter decided that the fastest way forward was, in fact,
straight through a wall.

It worked. Barely.

Loot for the session: a corroded ring, three potions of unknown make, and a
strong suspicion that something bigger lives further down.
